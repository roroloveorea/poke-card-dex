# PokeCardDex Catalog

PokeCardDex helps collectors identify an exact Pokémon card printing and understand its latest available indicative market price.

## Language

**Set**:
A named, language-specific release containing a defined checklist of card printings. English and Japanese releases remain separate sets even when they share a theme.
_Avoid_: Collection, series

**Catalog Scope**:
The English and Japanese Pokémon TCG sets and card printings presented by PokeCardDex.
_Avoid_: Every regional release, translated duplicate

**Card Printing**:
A specific card identified by its set and collector number. Cards with the same Pokémon name in different sets are different card printings.
_Avoid_: Card version, Pokémon

**Variant**:
A purchasable form of a card printing, such as normal, reverse holofoil, or holofoil. MVP variants are ungraded.
_Avoid_: Condition, printing

**Price Quote**:
The latest available indicative market value for one card variant, attributed to a price source, native currency, and observation time. English-market quotes use USD and Japanese-market quotes use JPY; a quote is not a guaranteed sale value.
_Avoid_: Card value, guaranteed price

**Price Source**:
The marketplace or data provider from which a price quote originates.
_Avoid_: Seller
